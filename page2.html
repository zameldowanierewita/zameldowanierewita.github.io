Public transport

SKM - Szybka Kolej Miejska
(Rapid Urban Railway)

You can easily get to the Old Town in Gdańsk, as well as other cities in the Tri-City - Sopot and Gdynia, the nearest station is SKM Politechnika - 5 minutes walk from the apartment.

PKM - Pomorska Kolej Metropolitalna
(Pomeranian Metropolitan Railway)

It can easily reach the AIRPORT or even more distant Pomeranian towns such as Kartuzy or Kościerzyna. The nearest PKM station is PKM Wrzeszcz - 25 minutes walk from the apartment.

Tram transport

The well-distributed tram lines in Gdańsk allow for convenient transport around the city, from the apartment you can easily reach the beach in Brzeźno with line 3 and line 5, which depart from the Wyspiańskiego stop, which is only a 5-minute walk from the apartment.
